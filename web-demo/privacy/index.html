Privacy Policy

PrivacyGate is designed as a local-first privacy layer that helps users review and protect sensitive business data before using external AI or automation services.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AI PM LAB PrivacyGate handles information when you use the website, desktop application, browser demo, and optional connected services.

2. Local-first processing

PrivacyGate is designed to perform sensitive-data detection, masking, restoration, and related document processing locally on your device where supported. Protected content and reversible mappings are intended to remain under your control unless you explicitly choose to use a connected external service.

3. Google Drive and Gmail access

If you connect a Google account, PrivacyGate may request permission to read selected Google Drive files or Gmail content for features you choose to use. PrivacyGate does not use these permissions to sell data, serve advertising, or build advertising profiles.

Google account data is accessed only to provide user-requested PrivacyGate functionality. The application does not modify or delete Gmail messages unless a future feature explicitly requests and receives the corresponding permission.

4. Data minimization

PrivacyGate is designed to minimize the amount of sensitive information exposed to third-party AI and automation services. Users are expected to review detected values and protected output before external sharing.

5. Website information

The public website may collect limited technical information required to operate the site, process contact requests, measure aggregate usage, or protect the service from abuse. Third-party services used for hosting, forms, or analytics are governed by their own privacy terms.

6. Retention

Local PrivacyGate data remains on the user's device according to application settings and user actions. Data sent to connected third-party services is subject to the retention practices of those services.

7. Security

PrivacyGate uses technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, but no software or networked system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may stop using PrivacyGate, disconnect connected accounts, remove local data, or revoke third-party account permissions at any time through the relevant application or provider controls.

9. Children

PrivacyGate is intended for professional and business use and is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to online services.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the product evolves. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current published version.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the contact methods provided on the PrivacyGate website.
